Privacy Policy for Digital Sticky Notes

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 | App Version: 2.7.5

Short Summary: Digital Sticky Notes is built with your privacy as a primary feature. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, notes, or usage information. Everything you create stays entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect zero personal or non-personal data.

Digital Sticky Notes operates entirely as an on-device utility. We do not use any servers, user accounts, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or tracking services. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or record your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.
  • No Device or Advertising IDs: We do not access or collect Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), IP addresses, IMEI numbers, or MAC addresses.
  • No Content Collection: Your sticky notes, text, checklist items, freehand drawings, and customizations are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No Usage Analytics or Crash Reporting: We do not use tools like Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, or Amplitude to monitor app activity or crash reports.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All data generated when using Digital Sticky Notes is saved exclusively in your device's local sandboxed storage (using Android's native local storage mechanisms). This includes:

  • The text and checklist items on your notes.
  • Your freehand drawings and hand-drawn strokes.
  • Your note positions, colors, fonts, washi tape styles, and folder stacking configurations.
  • App preference settings (such as Wall themes and Home Screen Widget preferences).

This data remains on your physical device at all times and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear the app's data through Android system settings.

3. Network Communications, Remote Notices & Backups

Digital Sticky Notes is built to operate fully offline. Your notes, drawings, and personal configurations remain entirely on your device and are never uploaded or synced to external servers.

Remote App Notices: The app performs periodic, read-only network requests to a static public endpoint (hosted on GitHub Pages) to check for official app announcements, changelogs, and community updates. These requests are completely anonymous: no personal data, notes, user accounts, or device identifiers are sent or recorded.

User-Initiated Feedback: When you voluntarily choose to contact support or send feedback from Settings, an email draft is prepared in your device's email client containing basic diagnostic details (such as app version, device model, and OS version) to assist with troubleshooting. This information is only transmitted if you explicitly choose to hit send in your email client.

Backups & Exports: When you use the Backup Export/Import or Save as Image features, the app uses your device's native file system and system share sheet. We do not automatically back up your data or intercept these files; you remain in full control of where your files are saved.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

We do not bundle any third-party SDKs that collect data. Specifically:

  • No Advertising Networks: The app contains no advertisements and integrates no ad SDKs.
  • No In-App Purchases or Payment Processors: The app does not process financial transactions.
  • No Push Notifications or Remote Trackers: The app does not request push notification tokens or download over-the-air tracking code from external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Digital Sticky Notes does not collect any data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Because we do not collect user contact information, we encourage you to review this policy periodically.
